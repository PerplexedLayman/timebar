TimeBar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

TimeBar is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no server, no user accounts of its own, and no analytics or advertising.

Information TimeBar accesses

When you connect a Google account, TimeBar accesses your Google Calendar through Google's API to:

  • show your calendars and events on your desktop
  • create, edit, move and delete events when you ask it to

How your information is stored

Your calendar data is requested while the app is running and is not saved by TimeBar. Your settings, such as which calendar appears on which track, and the sign-in tokens that keep you connected are stored in files on your own computer. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Your calendar data travels only between your computer and Google.

Sharing

TimeBar does not sell or share your data, and does not use it for advertising. The developer has no access to it.

Google API Services

TimeBar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

You can remove an account at any time in TimeBar's Settings, which deletes its sign-in token from your computer and asks Google to end its access. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
