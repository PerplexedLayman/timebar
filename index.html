TimeBar

Your day, across your desktop.

TimeBar is a Windows desktop app that shows your day as a timeline behind your windows. Your Google Calendar events sit along the bottom of the screen, with a line that moves to show the current time.

What it does

  • Shows events from one or more Google accounts, organised into tracks.
  • Creates, edits, moves and deletes events directly from the timeline.
  • Points out events that overlap and helps you move them to a free slot.
  • Works alongside your wallpaper and other desktop tools.

TimeBar is a personal project.
